隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 7 月 19 日 ｜ 适用应用：智贷计算器（SmartLoan Calc）

一、概述

智贷计算器是一款纯离线的个人贷款计算工具。我们将隐私保护作为核心设计原则：不收集、不上传、不追踪。您的全部数据仅存在于您的设备上。

二、我们不收集任何数据

  • 不收集任何个人信息、设备信息或使用数据；
  • 不上传：应用不发起任何网络请求，全部计算在本机完成（Android 发布版不含网络权限）；
  • 无账号：无需注册登录；
  • 无第三方 SDK：不含广告、统计、埋点或社交分享 SDK。

三、本地存储

您保存的贷款方案与计算历史记录仅存储于本机应用沙盒内（Hive 数据库与系统偏好存储），不会离开设备。卸载应用即彻底删除。您也可随时在应用内「我的 → 清除历史记录」手动删除。

四、导出与分享

还款计划表 PDF / CSV 文件仅在您主动点击分享时，经操作系统分享面板发出，接收方与内容由您完全控制。应用本身不会读取、上传或保留这些文件的任何副本。

五、系统权限

本应用不申请通讯录、定位、相机、麦克风、相册等任何敏感系统权限。

六、儿童隐私

本应用面向成年用户，不面向儿童，且不因任何用途收集任何人的数据。

七、政策变更

如未来版本引入任何网络功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们将更新本政策、更新生效日期，并在应用内以显著方式提示。

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 19, 2026 ｜ Applies to: SmartLoan Calc (智贷计算器)

1. Overview

SmartLoan Calc is a fully offline personal loan calculator. Privacy is a core design principle: we collect nothing, upload nothing, and track nothing. All of your data stays on your device.

2. We collect no data

  • No collection of personal, device, or usage data of any kind;
  • No upload: the app makes zero network requests and all computation happens on-device (the Android release build does not even declare the internet permission);
  • No account: no sign-up or login of any kind;
  • No third-party SDKs: no advertising, analytics, tracking, or social SDKs.

3. Local storage

Saved loan plans and calculation history are stored only inside the on-device app sandbox (Hive database and system preferences) and never leave your device. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently. You can also delete history anytime via “Me → Clear history” in the app.

4. Export and sharing

Repayment schedule PDF / CSV files leave the device only when you explicitly share them through the operating system's share sheet. The recipient and content are entirely under your control; the app never reads, uploads, or keeps copies of these files.

5. System permissions

The app requests no sensitive system permissions — no contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library access.

6. Children's privacy

The app is intended for adults, is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone for any purpose.

7. Changes to this policy

If any network feature is introduced or data handling changes in a future version, we will update this policy and its effective date, and provide prominent in-app notice.

8. Contact us

For any questions about this policy, reach us via “Me → About” in the app or at:
tangzheng202202@gmail.com

This policy is identical to the in-app “Me → Privacy Policy” page.
